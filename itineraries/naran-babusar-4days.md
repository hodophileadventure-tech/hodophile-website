# Naran & Babusar Top — 4 Days

Overview
- Region: Kaghan Valley, Naran, Lake Saif-ul-Malook, Babusar Top
- Difficulty: Easy–Moderate (road travel with short walks)
- Best time: May–October (Babusar Top often closed in winter due to snow)
- Typical start: Mansehra or Islamabad (drive via Mansehra)

Day 1 — Arrival in Naran
- Morning: Depart Mansehra/Islamabad early. Scenic drive through Balakot and Kaghan toward Naran (4–6 hours total driving depending on stops).
- Midday: Stop in Kaghan town for lunch and short rest. Continue to Naran.
- Afternoon: Arrive Naran; check into hotel or guesthouse. Walk around central market; visit the riverfront for views and photos.
- Evening: Early dinner and rest. Book local jeep for Lake Saif-ul-Malook if you plan to visit at dawn.

Day 2 — Saif-ul-Malook or Lulusar Lakes
- Option A — Saif-ul-Malook (popular):
  - Early morning: Drive (or jeep) to the Saif-ul-Malook parking area at Naran and then take a jeep to the jeep-terminal or walk/hire a guide for the 2–3 km uphill path.
  - Morning–Midday: Boat ride on Saif-ul-Malook (seasonal) or explore lake edge; short hikes to viewpoints (30–60 minutes).
  - Afternoon: Return to Naran; relax or explore local handicrafts.
- Option B — Lulusar & Dudipatsar access (alternative):
  - Day trip to Lulusar Lake followed by views of Dudipatsar approach (road and trekking options vary).

Day 3 — Babusar Top & Babusar Pass Drive
- Morning: Early departure toward Babusar Top via Naran–Kaghan route. Drive up to Babusar Top (passes and altitude may make for cooler weather and spectacular panoramas).
- Midday: Stop at Babusar top for photos and short walks. If road/weather allows, descend the other side toward Chilas (this becomes a long cross-valley transit) — note: this converts the trip to a longer point-to-point.
- Afternoon: Return to Naran/Kaghan area depending on route choice. If returning to Naran, stop at scenic viewpoints and small villages en route.
- Notes: Babusar Top altitude ~4,170 m (approx). Watch for altitude effects if sensitive.

Day 4 — Leisure & Return
- Morning: Relaxed morning in Naran; optional short hikes to nearby streams or market.
- Midday: Begin return drive toward Mansehra/Islamabad. Stop en route for lunch and photo breaks.
- Afternoon: Arrival back at Mansehra/Islamabad; trip ends.

Logistics & Practical Notes
- Transport: A sturdy car is fine for main roads; local jeeps required for some short stretches (Saif-ul-Malook approach). If crossing Babusar toward Chilas, ensure vehicle is suitable and driver experienced.
- Permits: No special permits for domestic travel, but parks and local businesses may charge entry/parking; keep cash.
- Safety: Babusar Top and Lulusar roads can be affected by snow/rockslides in shoulder seasons. Avoid night driving on narrow mountain stretches.
- Packing: Warm layers, windproof jacket, sunscreen, sunglasses, hat, walking shoes, personal meds, water, snacks, cash.
- Estimated budget (per person): Transport & jeep PKR 5,000–15,000; Accommodation PKR 3,000–10,000/night; Meals PKR 500–1,500/day.
- Extension ideas: Add a night in Chilas for cross-GB road access or extend to Fairy Meadows (requires planning and extra days).
