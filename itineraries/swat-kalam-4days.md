# Swat & Kalam — 4 Days

Overview
- Region: Swat Valley & Kalam (Ushu, Matiltan, Mahodand)
- Difficulty: Easy–Moderate (mostly road travel, one optional jeep/short hikes)
- Best time: May–October
- Typical start: Mingora / Saidu Sharif (or Islamabad with 4–6 hr drive)

Day 1 — Arrival, Mingora & Transfer to Kalam
- Morning: Arrive Mingora / Saidu Sharif. Quick visit to Swat Museum (45–60 minutes) and Mingora bazaar for snacks, cash, and mobile top-up.
- Late morning: Drive to Bahrain (45–75 min from Mingora). Short tea/refreshment stop and river-side photos.
- Afternoon: Continue uphill to Kalam (approx. 2–3 hours from Bahrain depending on road). Check into guesthouse or hotel in Kalam/Usi.
- Evening: Walk around Kalam bazaar, meet local guides; early dinner and rest.
- Accommodation: Guesthouse/hotel in Kalam.

Day 2 — Ushu & Matiltan; Short Hikes
- Morning: Drive to Ushu Forest (15–30 minutes from Kalam). Walk in pine forests and along streams; easy 30–60 minute nature walks.
- Midday: Head to Matiltan (short detour). Scenic viewpoints and glacier-fed streams; picnic lunch by the water.
- Afternoon: Optional short hike (30–90 minutes) to a nearby viewpoint or meadow; hire a local guide for off-path viewpoints.
- Evening: Return to Kalam for dinner.

Day 3 — Mahodand Lake (Full Day Excursion)
- Early morning: Depart for Mahodand Lake (1.5–2.5 hours by jeep depending on season/road). Hire a local 4x4 in Kalam; roads beyond Ushu are usually rough.
- Morning: Arrive at Mahodand Lake. Walk the shores, hire a wooden boat (if available), or try basic trout fishing (arranged locally).
- Midday: Lakeside picnic or lunch at a simple dhaba. Explore nearby alpine meadows.
- Afternoon: Optional short treks (1–3 hours) to smaller ponds or ridgelines for panoramic photos.
- Evening: Return to Kalam.
- Notes: Carry warm layers; weather changes quickly at higher altitude.

Day 4 — Return to Mingora / Departure
- Morning: Leisurely breakfast; last-minute shopping in Kalam.
- Midday: Drive back via Bahrain to Mingora (3–4 hours depending on stops). Optionally stop at Malam Jabba (adds time) for a chairlift/viewpoint.
- Afternoon: Arrival Mingora—drop-off at bus/road transport hub or continue onward to Islamabad.

Logistics & Practical Notes
- Transport: Private car or minivan recommended. For Mahodand, hire a local 4x4/jeep from Kalam.
- Permits: No special permits normally required for domestic travelers. Foreign visitors should carry passport copies and check current travel advisories.
- Safety: Roads can be narrow and prone to rockfalls/landslides in monsoon season; avoid travel in heavy rain. Keep emergency numbers and hotel contact.
- Packing: layered clothing, waterproof jacket, sturdy walking shoes, sunscreen, hat, basic first-aid, water bottle, power bank, cash (small denominations).
- Approximate budget (per person): Transport + guide/jeep PKR 8,000–20,000; Accommodation PKR 2,500–8,000/night; Meals PKR 500–2,000/day.
- Extension ideas: Add an overnight camp at Mahodand or continue to Gabral valley for multi-day trekking.
